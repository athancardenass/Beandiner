# Bean Diner QR Standee Design

## Goal

Prepare a client-facing, print-ready proposal for a tabletop QR standee that opens the Bean Diner website and clearly identifies each table.

## Approved direction

- Portrait standee, 150 × 200 mm finished size.
- Espresso-dark, scan-first look using Bean Diner's warm cream and tan accent colors.
- Bean Diner logo at the top, a short “Scan to explore our menu” call to action, and one large, high-contrast QR code in a cream panel.
- A distinct `TABLE 01` indicator, repeated through `TABLE 20`; each number gets its own page.
- Keep the composition clean and readable at tabletop distance. Preserve a clear QR quiet zone and avoid placing graphics over the QR.

## QR destination

The custom `beandiner.com` domain has not been purchased. For the proposal preview, encode the currently live `https://beandiner.vercel.app/` URL so the client can scan it. Do not print the temporary URL visibly. The final QR must be regenerated after the custom domain is active; the visual layout and table numbering can remain unchanged.

## Deliverables

- A 20-page PDF with finished trim size 150 × 200 mm and 3 mm bleed, one table number per page.
- A single-page client preview PNG with the standee mockup and a separate note that the QR destination is temporary.
- An editable vector source for future QR and domain replacement.

## Acceptance criteria

- All pages use the same layout and contain the correct unique table number from 01 to 20.
- The QR code scans to the temporary live site and has sufficient contrast and an unobstructed quiet zone.
- The PDF pages have consistent dimensions and the stated bleed.
- The client preview note says the QR currently uses the Vercel site and will be updated after `beandiner.com` is active; the standee itself does not show the temporary URL.
